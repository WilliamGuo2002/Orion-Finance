# Competitive Landscape: Investing-Focused Portfolio Trackers, Stock Research Apps and AI Investing Assistants (as of Nov 2026)

Context: competitors to "Orion Finance" (solo-dev iOS app for mainly Canadian retail investors: SnapTrade brokerage linking, Plaid bank balances, net worth chart, watchlist, movers, news, Gurus/13F tab, AI chat, personalized recommendations, portfolio builder, stock compare, paper trading). Research date: 2026-11-13. Many price figures come from third-party comparison/affiliate sites that disagree with each other; conflicts are flagged. Vendor pages were used where they could be fetched (Snowball, Getquin, Stock Events snippet).

## Key Question 1: Pricing, platforms, standout features, brokerage linking, Canadian broker support, CAD/USD and Canadian tax features

### Takeaway
The market splits into (a) dividend/performance trackers (Snowball, Sharesight, Getquin, Parqet, Delta, Stock Events), mostly free for about 10–15 holdings with paid plans around US$50–150/yr, which only weakly support Canadian brokers (usually file import, except IBKR/Questrade); (b) Canada-specific ACB/tax trackers (Wealthica, AllInvestView, AdjustedCostBase.ca, TraderSight), which own the "ACB + TFSA/RRSP + Bank of Canada FX" niche but are utilitarian; and (c) research platforms (Seeking Alpha, Simply Wall St, Morningstar, TipRanks, Koyfin, Fiscal.ai), priced at roughly US$120–$300+/yr, with no Canadian tax angle. None of the global trackers found combines direct Wealthsimple sync, ACB and an AI assistant.

### Inferences
- Orion's SnapTrade route (which can sync Wealthsimple directly) is a real advantage over Snowball, Sharesight and Portseido, which need file imports for Wealthsimple. It is not unique, though: AllInvestView and Wealthica already sync Wealthsimple and Questrade.
- The Canada-specific apps compete on tax correctness (ACB, superficial loss, BoC FX), not on design or AI. An app that pairs Canada-grade tax and CAD/USD handling with modern UX and AI looks like an open gap, but per-trade BoC FX and pooled ACB are hard to get right.
- Typical paid tiers cluster at about US$50–150/yr for trackers and US$250–300/yr for research platforms. A solo-dev app would likely need to land in the tracker band.
- 13F/guru data is commoditized (free on Dataroma/WhaleWisdom), so a Gurus tab is a retention feature, not a paid differentiator.

### Gaps
- Could not verify current official pricing for Sharesight, Seeking Alpha, TipRanks, Koyfin, Simply Wall St or Morningstar from vendor pages. The figures above are third-party and conflicting.
- No data found on Stocktwits (pricing, Canada) or Kubera's Canadian broker coverage, and only partial data on Parqet/Ziggma features.
- Snowball's monthly prices were not legible. Getquin's and Delta's Canadian broker coverage is not confirmed.
- App Store ratings and review counts were not collected for most apps (no reliable source retrieved).
- Whether Fey still exists as a standalone product after the acquisition is unconfirmed.

## Key Question 2: What broker-native apps (Wealthsimple, Questrade, IBKR, Robinhood) already provide

### Takeaway
Canadian brokers moved aggressively in 2026. Questrade (July 2026) and IBKR (June 2026) opened accounts to ChatGPT/Claude via MCP. Wealthsimple bought Fey (2025) and launched the "Penny" AI assistant (Nov 2026), which can trade and rebalance with user confirmation. Broker-native research and AI therefore now directly overlap with a third-party AI investing app.

### Cited Findings
- **Wealthsimple Penny** (announced Nov 5, 2026): handles e-transfers, bill payments, expense management and duplicate-charge flags. It can buy/sell securities and **auto-rebalance a portfolio**, executing generated orders after the user reviews them. ChatGPT and Claude connections are planned for early 2027, with trades sent back to Wealthsimple for approval. A new app is due in early 2027. Wealthsimple reported AUA of $169.2B (Sept 30, 2026) and 4M+ Canadian users. — [Advisor.ca](https://www.advisor.ca/industry-news/wealthsimple-embraces-ai-with-new-chatbot-connections-to-chatgpt-and-claude/)
- Penny "won't move money, place a trade or complete any other task without confirmation" and has no access to the outside internet. Broad availability is expected late 2026 or early 2027. — [CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/business/article/wealthsimple-launches-ai-banking-assistant-that-can-help-move-money-place-trades/); [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/?p=411820)
- Wealthsimple introduced "advanced investing tools" at its $100B milestone (Oct 2025). — [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/wealthsimple-hits-100-billion-milestone-unveils-new-suite-of-advanced-investing-tools)
- **Questrade** (July 29–30, 2026): "agentic finance." Its MCP integration lets AI assistants view and analyze the portfolio, research markets and draft trades that are confirmed in-app. "Flows" executes plain-language strategies. A planned "Balance Sheet" feature will aggregate holdings from other institutions, which is a direct net-worth-aggregation competitor. Sources conflict on supported assistants (Questrade says ChatGPT, Claude, Codex and Cursor; NCFA says the beta supports Claude/Claude Code). It also introduced $0 options. — [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/questrade-is-remaking-its-platform-for-an-ai-driven-investing-market/); [BNN press release](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/press-releases/2026/07/30/questrade-leads-new-age-of-financial-services-in-canada-unleashing-agentic-finance-0-options-and-more/); [NCFA](https://ncfacanada.org/questrade-mcp-ai-brokerage-accounts/); [Investment Executive](https://www.investmentexecutive.com/?p=113834)
- **Interactive Brokers** connected accounts to Claude, ChatGPT and Grok in June 2026; users research and prepare orders but approve every trade. — [NCFA](https://ncfacanada.org/questrade-mcp-ai-brokerage-accounts/)
- **Robinhood Cortex**: launched March 2025 with early access for Gold members (real-time analysis, trade suggestions, stock summaries). "Digests" (AI explanations of why a stock moved) rolled out in the US and then to all UK customers in Aug 2025, free for UK users. Not all securities are covered. — [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/25/03/44530226/robinhood-launches-ai-investment-tool-cortex-to-expand-market-analysis-access-a-premium-research-assistant-right-); [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/08/19/robinhood-launches-stock-picking-ai-summaries/); [Robinhood UK help](https://robinhood.com/gb/en/support/articles/cortex-digests)
- **Public.com**: Alpha AI research assistant (GPT-4, May 2023). "Generated Assets" (May 14, 2025) turns a thematic prompt into a custom index benchmarked against the S&P 500, with 2,500+ created in a month. A hedge-portfolio version (Apr 2026) can route options/futures orders. Public is moving to an "agentic brokerage." Its disclosures say output "is not an investment recommendation or advice." — [Finextra](https://finextra.com/pressarticle/96889/public-taps-gpt-4-for-investment-research-tool); [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/public-debuts-ai-powered-index-generator-benchmarking-sp-500/); [Pulse](https://www.pulse.bot/options-derivatives/news/public-unveils-ai-generated-assets-tool-for-custom-hedge-portfolios-90fb928a-283e-4864-b4b1-3a96e0aaebc9/); [FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/?p=37131)

### Inferences
- Wealthsimple's Penny, the Fey integration and the planned 2027 app, plus Questrade's Balance Sheet, together cover much of Orion's pitch for single-broker users: AI chat over the portfolio, research, news, rebalancing and aggregation. Orion's defensible ground narrows to **multi-broker plus bank aggregation in one view**, broker-neutral research, Gurus, paper trading and education, aimed at users who hold accounts at several institutions.
- Because Questrade and IBKR now expose MCP to ChatGPT/Claude, power users can already "chat with my Questrade portfolio" for free inside general-purpose assistants.
- Robinhood is not available to Canadians (general knowledge, not sourced here), so Cortex mainly sets user expectations rather than competing directly.

### Gaps
- No details found on BMO InvestorLine, TD Direct Investing, RBC DI or CIBC Investor's Edge native analytics or AI features.
- Robinhood Gold price and the US availability tier of Cortex as of late 2026 were not confirmed.
- No information on whether Wealthsimple restricts SnapTrade-type aggregator access (a key platform risk for Orion).

## Key Question 3: Which apps use AI and how, and how users/reviewers perceive it

### Takeaway
AI shows up in three forms: (1) **summaries/explanations** (Robinhood Digests, Simply Wall St Charlie, Stock Events' rationed AI messages); (2) **chat over your linked portfolio** (Perplexity Finance and ChatGPT via Plaid, Getquin AI agents, Rallies.ai, Magnifi, Wealthsimple Penny, Questrade/IBKR via MCP, Snowball via MCP); and (3) **idea generation / thematic baskets** (Public Generated Assets, Fey/Fiscal natural-language screening). General-purpose assistants with Plaid linking (May 2026) have commoditized "chat with my portfolio." Sentiment favors AI that speeds up research and cites sources, and distrusts opaque predictions. Sentiment evidence is thin and mostly secondary.

### Cited Findings
- **ChatGPT**: preview launched May 15, 2026 for US ChatGPT Pro users, linking bank, brokerage and credit-card accounts via Plaid (12,000+ institutions incl. Schwab, Fidelity, Robinhood). Works on web and iOS. Cannot see full account numbers or make changes. Plaid cites 200M+ monthly users asking ChatGPT personal-finance questions. Coverage raises privacy concerns because the chatbot also shows ads. — [OpenAI help](https://help.openai.com/articles/20001222-finances-in-chatgpt); [Plaid blog](https://plaid.com/blog/chatgpt-personal-finance-plaid/); [American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/openai-launches-personal-finance-tools-for-chatgpt-pro-users); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-chatgpt-personal-finance-plaid)
- **Perplexity Finance**: Portfolio tab linking brokerage accounts via Plaid (around March 2026), later extended to bank, credit and loan accounts. One source says US **and Canadian** users can connect. Perplexity also supports remote MCP connectors, which third parties like Truthifi use. A June 2026 guide claims it does not connect brokerages, which is likely outdated. — [CFOtech](https://cfotech.co.uk/story/plaid-perplexity-launch-ai-powered-portfolio-tool); [Plaid blog](https://plaid.com/blog/powering-intellligent-finance-with-perplexity/); [Truthifi](https://truthifi.com/education/perplexity-finance-vs-truthifi); [ai2.work](https://ai2.work/blog/perplexity-and-plaid-build-an-ai-powered-financial-command-center); contradicted by [Walnut Invest](https://walnutinvest.com/resources/perplexity-finance-for-investing)
- Sources differ on the launch date. One says Perplexity added the Plaid integration May 14, 2026, one day before ChatGPT. — [American Banker / roundup](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/openai-launches-personal-finance-tools-for-chatgpt-pro-users); vs. March 2026 per [CFOtech](https://cfotech.co.uk/story/plaid-perplexity-launch-ai-powered-portfolio-tool)
- **Rallies.ai**: AI research app that links brokerages, answers plain-English questions and runs monitoring agents. "Everything is free today." It has MCP support. — [Walnut Invest](https://walnutinvest.com/resources/best-ai-investing-apps-for-experienced-investors); [aistockpickerapps](https://aistockpickerapps.com/best/ai-portfolio-trackers)
- **Magnifi** (TIFIN): conversational search/compare/track across accounts. Pricing conflicts ($13.99/mo; $8.25/mo annual or $14/mo; $99/yr). A Google Play reviewer called it "more like an old chat bot for FAQs," and roundups say its answers "can miss the mark on more specific questions." — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.magnifi.app&hl=en_US); [agent-finder](https://agent-finder.co/reviews/magnifi); [Barebone AI](https://barebone.ai/resources/best-ai-investing-apps)
- **Fiscal.ai (formerly FinChat)**: web-first fundamentals terminal, not a native mobile app. Free tier; Pro about $39/mo annual or $49 monthly; Max $79/mo. — [diyai](https://diyai.io/ai-tools/finance/best-ai-investing-apps/); [TraderHQ FinChat review](https://traderhq.com/finchat-review-ai-financial-assistant-smart-investors/)
- **Getquin**: AI portfolio analysis in Premium, and "unlimited AI financial agents" in Wealth (€149.99/yr). — [Getquin pricing](https://www.getquin.com/pricing/)
- **Stock Events**: AI chat rationed to 5 messages/24h on the free tier, which uses AI as an upsell lever. — [Stock Events pricing](https://stockevents.app/pricing)
- **Snowball**: no in-app chat; it exposes an MCP server (Expert tier) so users bring their own Claude/Cursor. — [Snowball pricing](https://snowball-analytics.com/pricing)
- **Simply Wall St** "Charlie AI" screens, monitors and flags risks. **TipRanks** has "Samuel AI" chat. Morningstar's AI is described as "more research tool than assistant" (assistant last described 2024). Koyfin's AI is inconsistently reported. — [Stocksimplifier](https://stocksimplifier.com/compare/simply-wall-st-vs-seeking-alpha); [PageCrawl](https://pagecrawl.io/blog/best-investment-research-tools); [Walnut Invest](https://walnutinvest.com/resources/best-ai-portfolio-analytics-platforms)
- **Perception**: per a secondary summary of 2026 Reddit discussions, users favor AI that speeds research, and trust "drops quickly" when tools make opaque predictions or imply that output replaces verification; citations don't prevent errors. — [diyai Reddit roundup](https://diyai.io/ai-tools/reddit/best-ai-finance-tools-on-reddit/)
- A G2 analysis (2025) found that mentions of AI inaccuracy fell across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity in Mar–Oct 2025, but trust favors models that show sources. — [G2](https://learn.g2.com/tech-signals-ai-hallucinations-and-research)
- Older data (2023): 47% of Americans said they had used ChatGPT for stock tips (Motley Fool survey), and over a third said they would trust ChatGPT-like software for investment advice (Magnifi survey). — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2023/05/19/chatgpt-why-take-its-investing-advice); [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/z/30947444)
- UK FCA data chief: only 40% of consumers understand they have no recourse if general-purpose AI advice goes wrong. — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/regulation/fca-asks-ai-firms-to-warn-users-about-the-limits-of-chatbot-financial-advice/)
- An AI stock-picker experiment was covered sceptically (2024). — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2024/09/25/ai_stock_picker/)

### Inferences
- A standalone "AI chat about investing" is now table stakes and free in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Rallies and broker apps. Differentiation has to come from grounding: answers tied to the user's actual multi-broker holdings in CAD, Canadian account types (TFSA/RRSP/FHSA) and tax rules, with visible sources and no black-box "buy" calls.
- Orion's "personalized stock recommendations by risk profile" sits in the riskiest perception zone (opaque predictions) and the riskiest regulatory zone (see Key Question 5). Framing it as screening or educational ideas with citations is safer.
- Stock Events' metered AI messages and Getquin's AI-in-premium tiering show a working monetization pattern: free AI with a cap, unlimited AI behind a paywall.

### Gaps
- No rigorous 2026 survey found on retail trust in AI investing tools; no documented hallucination incidents found for specific apps (Cortex, Magnifi, etc.).
- AI features of Seeking Alpha, Yahoo Finance (only a Taiwan "AI Stock Pick Summary" VIP feature, June 2026), Koyfin and Stocktwits were not confirmed. — [kocpc repost](https://en.kocpc.com.tw/?p=3097)

## Key Question 4: Design/UX reputation

### Takeaway
Fey was the design benchmark in investing ("Bloomberg meets Apple design") and is now part of Wealthsimple, whose own app is also design-led. Most trackers (Wealthica, AllInvestView, Sharesight) are utilitarian. Getquin is praised for its "cool design." Evidence on other apps' UX is thin.

## Key Question 5: Trends 2024–2026 (consolidation, pricing, AI launches, regulation)

### Takeaway
Between 2024 and 2026: brokers absorbed research startups (Wealthsimple–Fey, eToro–Delta) and launched native AI. General-purpose AI gained Plaid account linking (May 2026). Brokers opened MCP access (IBKR June, Questrade July 2026), while Canadian banks blocked third-party AI agents (Oct 2026). Free tiers tightened (Delta Jan 2026). Regulation is mostly guidance (CSA SN 11-348 in Dec 2024; SEC scrutiny and congressional letters in 2026), with no new AI-advice-specific rule found.

### Inferences
- Platform risk for Orion: the bank blocks target credential-sharing AI agents, not necessarily Plaid's API/OAuth aggregation, but the climate is tightening. Orion's Plaid bank-balance feature and SnapTrade brokerage links depend on institutions that may restrict third-party access. Monitor this.
- Regulatory risk: "personalized stock recommendations by interests/risk profile" for Canadians could look like suitability-based advice, which in Canada is a registrable activity. The CSA's stance on black-box AI points to disclaimers, explainability and keeping recommendations generic or educational. (This is an inference, not legal advice; registration thresholds were not researched.)
- Consolidation pattern: successful independent investing apps get acquired by brokers (Fey to Wealthsimple, Delta to eToro). That is a possible exit path, and also a sign that brokers are absorbing the third-party feature set.
