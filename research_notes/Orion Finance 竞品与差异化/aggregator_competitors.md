# Competitive Landscape: Net Worth / Personal Finance Aggregator Apps for Canadian (and US) Investors (as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-13. Source caveat: many Canada-focused comparison pages are written by competing apps (Mozaic Finance, Unified, Greenline, Waypoint Budget, Richify), and they disclose this. Their facts about competitors are usually dated and quoted from official help centres, but treat their rankings and claims about themselves as marketing. Official vendor pages were fetched where possible. Monarch's help centre returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch, so its statements are cited through search snippets and secondary quotes.

## Q1. Per-app profile: pricing, platforms, core features, aggregation provider, investment depth, multi-currency

### Takeaway
The big US apps (Monarch, Copilot, Empower, Rocket Money, YNAB, Simplifi) are USD-first. They either don't serve Canada at all (Copilot, Rocket Money, Empower in practice) or serve it with single-currency handling and shallow investment tracking (Monarch, YNAB, Simplifi). Typical paid pricing is US$95–120/yr, with Copilot moving up to US$109–119/yr in Nov 2026. The Canada-native options are Wealthica (deep investment/ACB reporting, now paid only, C$50–250/yr), Wealthsimple's free in-app net-worth tab, and a wave of small Plaid + SnapTrade indie apps. Mozaic Finance is architecturally almost identical to Orion.

Orion's differentiation therefore has to come from the investing-research layer (watchlist, news, famous-investor portfolios, AI chat, recommendations, paper trading), per-institution charts and privacy mode, none of which the aggregators above emphasize.
- Price anchors for a Canadian paid tier are about C$50–150/yr: Wealthica Connect at $50, Mozaic at C$99, Monarch at about C$140, and Copilot now about US$109–119.
- Wealthsimple's free net-worth tab plus the Penny AI assistant is a structural threat for users whose assets are mostly at Wealthsimple. Its weakness is that external accounts are balances-only and may not auto-refresh.

## Q2. Canadian support quality: institutions, CAD/USD, TFSA/RRSP/FHSA

### Takeaway
Only Canada-native tools (Wealthica, Mozaic, and in a limited way Wealthsimple) reliably connect Canadian brokerages and handle CAD/USD properly. US apps either exclude Canada (Copilot, Rocket Money, Empower) or connect Canadian banks via Plaid with USD-first, no-conversion currency handling (Monarch, YNAB, Simplifi). Explicit TFSA/RRSP/FHSA features are rare in every product reviewed. Canadian connections in general are fragile because open banking isn't yet live.

### Cited Findings
- Monarch connects the big six plus Tangerine and Simplii via Plaid, with gaps for smaller credit unions and Equitable Bank's HISA. Brokerages are balances-only, and net worth flattens to one base currency with no daily FX. — [Mozaic: Mint alternatives](https://mozaicfinance.com/blog/best-mint-alternatives-canada-2026/). Another source says coverage is "uneven via Plaid". — [WealthNorth](https://wealthnorth.ca/personal-finance/budgeting/best-budgeting-apps-canada/). A competitor reports Monarch's help centre noting some Canadian banks are hard to keep synced and suggesting manual accounts. — [Waypoint Budget](https://waypointbudget.com/blog/waypoint-vs-monarch-money-couples-canada-2026)
- One ranking recommends Monarch for Canadians only "if all accounts are in CAD". — [Richify CA](https://www.richify.ai/ca/best-budgeting-apps-canada)
- Copilot's help centre says it supports US-based institutions only, and it isn't in the Canadian App Store as of Sept 2026 (competitor source). — [Waypoint: Copilot alternative](https://waypointbudget.com/copilot-alternative). An open feature request asks for non-US banks. — [Copilot feedback board](https://feedback.copilot.money/feature-requests/p/international-support). Canada support was reportedly promised in 2022 and still hadn't shipped by mid-2026. — [Mozaic: Mint alternatives](https://mozaicfinance.com/blog/best-mint-alternatives-canada-2026/)
- Empower links only US institutions. — [Empower support](https://support-personalwealth.empower.com/hc/en-us/articles/201169680-Can-I-link-an-international-account-in-Empower-Personal-Dashboard). Rocket Money is US-only. — [Unified](https://unifiedbankings.com/blog/rocket-money-canada)
- Simplifi connects BMO, Scotiabank, CIBC, RBC and TD (since its Aug 2024 Canada launch). — [Unified](https://unifiedbankings.com/blog/rocket-money-canada)
- YNAB connects Canada via Plaid/MX but calls it "more challenging", with one currency per plan. — [Mozaic](https://mozaicfinance.com/blog/best-personal-finance-app-canada-2026/)
- Wealthica's institution list covers TD (EasyWeb/WebBroker), RBC, BMO InvestorLine, CIBC, Scotia iTRADE, National Bank, Desjardins, Tangerine, EQ Bank, Simplii, Questrade (OAuth), Wealthsimple (OAuth) and IBKR (Flex Query). The review notes Wealthsimple Trade is not supported. — [Unified](https://unifiedbankings.com/blog/wealthica-review). A competing Canadian app says Wealthica's Canadian aggregator connections can drop "every 24 to 48 hours" (self-interested claim). — [Greenline vs Wealthica](https://usegreenline.com/compare/wealthica). Wealthica reports currency gains and losses in its ACB capital-gains reports. — [Unified](https://unifiedbankings.com/blog/wealthica-review). Strong TFSA and RRSP support is attributed to Wealthica. — [WealthRocket](https://www.wealthrocket.com/investing/wealthica-review/); [money.ca](https://money.ca/investing/net-worth/net-worth-trackers)
- Mozaic's SnapTrade route covers Wealthsimple, Questrade, IBKR Canada and TD Direct Investing. Scotia iTrade, NBDB, Qtrade and Disnat are unreachable. — [Mozaic](https://mozaicfinance.com/blog/best-personal-finance-app-canada-2026/)
- Open banking: Wealthsimple's help content reportedly says open banking "is not yet in place in Canada" (paraphrased by Mozaic). — [Mozaic](https://mozaicfinance.com/blog/best-personal-finance-app-canada-2026/). The consumer-driven banking rules are described as still "proposed". — [NCFA Canada](https://ncfacanada.org/?p=84234)
- Multi-currency is the main gap in US apps for CAD+USD holders. — [money.ca roundup / search set](https://money.ca/investing/net-worth/net-worth-trackers); [Richify CA](https://www.richify.ai/ca/best-budgeting-apps-canada)

### Inferences
- A correct CAD/USD toggle with real FX conversion is a genuine differentiator against Monarch, YNAB, Simplifi and Copilot. It isn't one against Mozaic, Wealthica or Kubera.
- SnapTrade's Canadian gaps (Scotia iTrade, NBDB, Qtrade, Disnat), as reported for Mozaic, will likely apply to Orion too. Wealthica's credential-based connectors cover some of these (Scotia iTRADE, BMO InvestorLine), which is a coverage advantage for Wealthica.
- No product surfaced explicit FHSA tracking or TFSA/RRSP contribution-room features. This looks like an open niche, but it's unverified (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No source confirmed explicit FHSA support in any app. TFSA/RRSP-specific features beyond account labelling weren't documented for Monarch, Simplifi, Lunch Money or Kubera.
- Official supported-institution lists for Monarch and Simplifi in Canada (EQ Bank, Wealthsimple, Questrade, IBKR) weren't retrieved.
- No authoritative date was found for Canada's consumer-driven banking go-live.

## Q3. Strengths and weaknesses as reviewers describe them

### Takeaway
Reviewers praise Monarch and Copilot for polish and UI, Empower for free investment analytics, YNAB for budgeting methodology, PocketSmith for forecasting, and Wealthica for Canadian investment and tax reporting. The recurring weaknesses are USD-only handling, connection reliability for Canadian institutions, shallow investment analytics in budgeting apps, and weak budgeting in investment trackers.

### Cited Findings
- Monarch: "most polished interface" for US or Canadian users, but pricier. — [money.ca / net worth roundup](https://money.ca/investing/net-worth/net-worth-trackers). Mature Mint-like features, a good mobile app and full CSV export. Weaknesses are US data residency, weak multi-currency, English only and weak investment tools. — [Mozaic: Mint alternatives](https://mozaicfinance.com/blog/best-mint-alternatives-canada-2026/)
- Copilot: polished UI and strong categorization. No free tier, no Android, and household sharing wasn't live at the time of review. — [Mozaic: Mint alternatives](https://mozaicfinance.com/blog/best-mint-alternatives-canada-2026/); [Walletgrower](https://walletgrower.com/blog/copilot-money-review-2026)
- Empower: the most capable free option, with the trade-off of advisory upselling. — [Richify net worth](https://www.richify.ai/best/net-worth-trackers)
- YNAB: zero-based envelope budgeting, mature. Overkill if you just want visibility. — [Mozaic: Mint alternatives](https://mozaicfinance.com/blog/best-mint-alternatives-canada-2026/)
- Lunch Money: Canadian-built, real API, strong customization. The mobile app is weaker than the web app. — [Mozaic: Mint alternatives](https://mozaicfinance.com/blog/best-mint-alternatives-canada-2026/)
- PocketSmith: decades-long forecasting scenarios, but a busy UI and steep learning curve. — [Mozaic: Mint alternatives](https://mozaicfinance.com/blog/best-mint-alternatives-canada-2026/)
- Wealthica: deep Canadian brokerage coverage and ACB, income, fee and performance reports. Weaknesses are no longer being free for synced accounts, basic budgeting, credential-based connections, a web-first design and an unstated pricing currency. — [Unified](https://unifiedbankings.com/blog/wealthica-review)
- Wealthsimple tab: free and convenient if your money is already there. Outside accounts show balances only. — [Mozaic: Mint alternatives](https://mozaicfinance.com/blog/best-mint-alternatives-canada-2026/). Not designed as a cross-institution aggregator (competitor claim). — [Richify vs Wealthsimple](https://www.richify.ai/ca/compare/richify-vs-wealthsimple)
- Mozaic (self-described): consolidated net worth and Canadian data residency, but not a zero-based budget tool and no Android. — [Mozaic: Mint alternatives](https://mozaicfinance.com/blog/best-mint-alternatives-canada-2026/)

### Inferences
- No reviewed aggregator combines aggregation with investing research such as news, famous-investor 13F portfolios, recommendations and paper trading. These are features of research apps rather than of aggregators, which supports Orion's positioning as "aggregator + research" for Canadians.

### Gaps
- No independent major-outlet reviews (Wirecutter, The Verge, MoneySense) were retrieved within the tool budget. Most cited reviews come from competitors or affiliate blogs.

## Q4. Recent changes 2024–2026 and where Mint users went

### Takeaway
2024 was defined by Mint's shutdown (Mar 23, 2024) and Hardbacon's collapse (Aug 2024), which left Canadians without a free mainstream aggregator. Simplifi entered Canada in Aug 2024, and Wealthica dropped its free sync tier (Nov 2023). In 2026 prices rose (Tiller to $99, Copilot to $109–119), Monarch formally marketed itself in Canada, and AI assistants arrived: Wealthsimple Penny and Claude's US-only Plaid-based finance hub, both in Nov 2026.

### Inferences
- Orion competes with two AI threats. One is brokerage-native AI (Wealthsimple Penny, which can act on accounts). The other is general LLMs with direct aggregation (Claude, not yet in Canada). Orion's AI chat has to stand out through cross-institution context plus investing research, not generic Q&A.
- The rising US price anchors (US$99–250/yr) leave room for an indie Canadian app to price in CAD at or below C$100/yr, as Mozaic does.
